    NO STEMMING OR LEMMATIZATION. Khmer is analytic - verbs do not conjugate
    and nouns do not inflect, so there are no affixes to strip. Applying an
    English stemmer would corrupt the text without removing anything real.

[2] WHY SEGMENTATION IS THE HARD STEP
    Khmer writes without spaces between words. This sentence:
      បឹងកេតធ្លាប់បានក្លាយជាជើងឯកនៃពានរង្វាន់សម្តេចតេជោនេះ១លើកកាលពីឆ្នាំ២០១៩
    contains 3 whitespace-separated chunks but
    27 actual words once segmented:
      ['បឹង', 'កេត', 'ធ្លាប់', 'បាន', 'ក្លាយជា', 'ជើងឯក', 'នៃ', 'ពានរង្វាន់', 'សម្តេច', 'តេជោ', 'នេះ', '១លើក', 'កាលពី', 'ឆ្នាំ']

    Splitting on spaces would give a handful of enormous 'words', each
    appearing once, and every downstream count would be meaningless.
